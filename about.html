About Urban Latin History

Urban Latin was born from the idea of a programmer just beginning his journey, with a passion for diverse food, creativity, and style. What started as a simple idea became a project that brings together the best of three different worlds.

Our goal is to create an experience where food, culture, and modern style come together in one place. We represent creativity, diversity, and a passion for sharing unique flavors inspired by Latin culture.

Born from passion and built with creativity, it officially launched on September 11, 2026, in Lehi, Utah.

Welcome to a place where flavor, culture, and style come together.
